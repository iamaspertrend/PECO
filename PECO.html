THE THINGS WE DON'T NOTICE

A pen is one of the smallest things we use every day. We pick it up, write a few words, put it down and eventually throw it away.

But what happens after the last word?

Conventional plastic pens are designed for convenience. But convenience doesn't always consider what happens when the product reaches the end of its useful life.

Every discarded pen is a reminder that even the smallest everyday objects can become part of a much bigger waste problem.
